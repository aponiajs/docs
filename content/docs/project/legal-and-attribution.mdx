[source license](https://github.com/aponiajs/aponiajs/blob/main/LICENSE) for the
authoritative terms.

Dependencies and third-party assets retain their own licenses and attribution
requirements.

## Project artwork

The site ships no character artwork. The only brand asset is the AponiaJS
mark in `public/brand`, which is project-owned and requires no third-party
credit.

Third-party artwork previously kept in this repository has been removed. If
any is reintroduced, it must carry its creator, publisher, rights-holder
context, source URL, and usage requirements alongside the file.
