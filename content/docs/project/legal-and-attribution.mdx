[source license](https://github.com/aponiajs/aponiajs/blob/main/LICENSE) for the
authoritative terms.

requirements.

## Original project artwork

AponiaJS-owned generated illustrations and the canonical AponiaJS-chan mascot
were generated in-house with OpenAI image generation for the project. This
project-owned artwork does not require an on-page third-party credit.

AponiaJS-chan is an original project mascot and does not use a third-party
character design. The canonical spelling is `AponiaJS-chan`; the earlier
`ApoliaJS-chan` spelling and assets are superseded.

## Historical third-party artwork

Historical third-party artwork files may remain in the documentation
repository. Their creator, publisher, rights-holder context, source URL, and
usage requirements are retained in the
[artwork source record](https://github.com/aponiajs/docs/blob/main/ARTWORK_SOURCES.md).

Do not remove those records while the corresponding files remain. Any reuse
must preserve the documented credit and source link.
